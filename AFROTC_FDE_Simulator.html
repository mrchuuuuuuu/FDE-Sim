SIMULATOR USER GUIDE

Lead the flight

1. Choose Practice or Evaluation, then click Report in and Start. Evaluation starts a five-minute countdown.

2. Type a command such as Forward, MARCH, click its shortcut, or use Voice. The flight moves on the map. When marching, watch the active heel-strike indicator.

3. Accomplish the 20 checklist movements, stay inside the field, then Report out. Use the After-action Review to score observable qualities with a human evaluator.

4. Expand the Route Planner to save and run sequences, or load a demonstration.

Tip: Practice mode pauses the timer but not your learning. If a command is rejected, follow the feedback describing the required state.
